# VFY-010 sensitivity: p08-tenancy-smoke.mjs against a deliberate tenancy fault

## Fault injected (temporary, reverted)

In apps/api/src/routes/authorization.rs, authorize_org stopped consulting the membership
table: a principal with no membership in the organization was given a synthetic
owner membership instead of the 404 the product uses. That is precisely the defect
VI-TEN-001 exists to detect.

## Why this matters for reading the number

16/18 proven with 0 leaks is only evidence because the probe reports 15 leaks and
exits 1 under the fault. One route stayed green under it (data_governance
/deletions, whose 404 comes from a second guard), which is why the figure is 15 and
not 18 -- a probe that caught all of them would be worth less than one that
catches them for the right reason.
